Fledgling Films is a passion project with the goal of posting post a video/sketch/short film every week for the rest of time itself.


Being creative is hard, but when the next video is only a week away it's easier to just learn something new and move on.


The mission for Fledgling Films is to create a passionate video community where we can share things we've made, things we want to make, goals, obstacles, anything! Just keep it friendly. We're all here for the same reason.
