Now, I know what you're thinking:

What the HECK is Fledgling Films?

Fledgling Films is a passion project of mine to post a video/sketch/short film every week for a full calendar year (at least)!

Being creative is hard, but when the next video is only a week away, there's less pressure to make this one absolutely perfect.

My hope is for Fledgling Films to become a small and passionate online video community where we can share things we've made, things we want to make, goals, obstacles, anything! Just keep it friendly. We're all here for the same reason.

Now go check out the many things this site has to offer :)
